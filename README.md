# Fynex Music Privacy Policy
Privacy Policy – Fynex Music Bot

**Effective Date:** October 27, 2026
**Last Updated:** October 27, 2026

---

# 1. Overview

Fynex Music ("Fynex", "the Bot", "we", "us") is a Discord music bot. It plays music in your server's voice channels and offers playlists, liked songs, audio filters, server settings, and some utility commands.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Fynex processes, why, and what choices you have. By adding or using Fynex, you agree to this Privacy Policy and our Terms & Conditions.

---

# 2. Information We Collect

What Fynex processes depends on the features that you or your server use.

## 2.1 Discord Information

- User IDs, Server (guild) IDs, Channel IDs, Role IDs, and Message IDs
- Your Discord username, display name, and avatar, when a command shows them (for example profile, userinfo, or avatar)
- Voice channel details, so the Bot knows where to join and play music

## 2.2 Server Settings

To work correctly in your server, Fynex may store settings such as:

- Server prefix
- DJ role
- 24/7 mode and the voice channel it uses
- Ignored text channels and voice channels
- Music log channel and setup channel
- Preset, source, engine, and customization settings
- Voice channel status and other configuration options

## 2.3 User Data and Content

If you use these features, Fynex may store:

- **Playlists:** playlist names, the tracks in them, and collaboration and sharing details. Playlists can be shared with others or made available to other users (for example through playlist explore, trending, or collab features).
- **Liked songs:** tracks you like using the like commands.
- **Listening history and statistics:** tracks played and usage counts that power commands such as history, myhistory, musicstats, topserver, topchannel, and recommendation features.
- **Saved queues:** queues you save to load later.
- **Personal settings:** for example your personal prefix, and your no-prefix status if enabled for you.
- **AFK status:** your AFK status and message, if you use the afk command.
- **Reviews and suggestions:** text you send through the review and suggestion commands.
- **Vote and premium information:** vote counts and reminders, and premium or redeem code status linked to your User ID.

## 2.4 Spotify Account Linking (Optional)

If you choose to link your Spotify account using the Spotify commands, Fynex processes the Spotify information needed to provide those features, such as your profile, your playlists, and your liked songs. This may include an authorization token from Spotify. Linking is optional. You can remove the link at any time with the Spotify unlink or logout commands. You can also revoke access in your Spotify account settings.

## 2.5 Messages and Commands

- Fynex supports both slash commands and prefix commands. To detect prefix commands, the Bot reads messages in channels it can see, using Discord's Message Content intent. Messages that are not commands are ignored.
- Command text (for example a song name or link) is used to carry out the command.
- Some features may keep a small amount of recent message data for a limited time, for example the snipe command may temporarily keep recently deleted messages so they can be shown.

## 2.6 Voice

Fynex only plays audio into voice channels. It does not record or listen to voice conversations.

## 2.7 Technical and Diagnostic Information

Fynex may keep error logs and basic diagnostic information (such as command errors, timestamps, and Server or User IDs related to an error) to fix bugs and keep the Bot working.

# 3. Information We Do Not Collect

Fynex does not ask for or store your email address, phone number, passwords, or payment card details. If you pay for premium, payments are handled by the payment provider and not by the Bot.

---

# 4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Play music and run commands
- Apply your server and personal settings
- Provide playlists, likes, history, stats, and recommendations
- Provide premium, vote, and Spotify features
- Prevent abuse, spam, and misuse of the Bot
- Fix bugs, keep the Bot stable, and improve it

We do not use your information for advertising.

---

# 5. Music Sources and Third-Party Services

Fynex finds and streams music from third-party platforms. Depending on the source you use, this can include YouTube, YouTube Music, Spotify, JioSaavn, Gaana, Apple Music, Amazon Music, Deezer, SoundCloud, Tidal, Bandcamp, Audiomack, Twitch, Mixcloud, VK Music, Pandora, Qobuz, Yandex Music, Anghami, Boomplay, Hungama, Jamendo, Archive.org, and other websites.

When you search for a song or give a link, the search text or link is sent to the relevant platform or to a service that helps us find the audio. These platforms are not run by us and have their own terms and privacy policies. We are not responsible for how they handle information.

Some utility and recommendation commands (such as lyrics, translate, google, and smart recommendation features) may also use third-party services.

Fynex also uses service providers such as hosting and database providers to run the Bot. They only handle data to provide those services.

Fynex runs on Discord and is also subject to the [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

# 6. Age-Restricted Content

Some music sources that Fynex can connect to may contain content that is not suitable for children. Server admins are responsible for how sources are used in their server. See our Terms & Conditions for more details.

# 7. Data Sharing

Fynex does not sell your data and does not trade it for advertising.

We may share limited information only:

- With service providers that help us run the Bot (such as hosting and database providers)
- With the third-party platforms mentioned above, to the extent needed to play the music you request
- When required by law, or to protect the Bot, its users, or others from abuse or harm

Some information is visible to other users by design. For example, playlists you share or make public, your Fynex profile, and stats such as top server or top channel results can be seen by others.

---

# 8. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep information for as long as it is needed to provide the features you use, or until you or your server asks for it to be removed. Diagnostic logs are kept only as long as needed to fix issues.

You can remove much of your data yourself:

- Delete a playlist with the playlist delete command
- Remove liked songs with the removelike and clearlikes commands
- Unlink Spotify with the Spotify unlink or logout commands
- Reset your personal settings, where the Bot allows it
- Remove the Bot from your server at any time

To request deletion of any other data linked to your User ID or Server ID, contact us using the details below. Please include your Discord User ID and, if relevant, your Server ID. We may ask for more information to confirm the request.

---

# 9. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information the Bot stores, and we follow Discord's API security practices. However, no online service can promise complete security.

# 10. Children's Privacy

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old (or older in some countries). Fynex follows the same rule and is not meant for children under that age. If we learn that we have collected data from someone under the required age, we will delete it.
